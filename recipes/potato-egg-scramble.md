# Potato and egg scramble

## Ingredients

<div class="ingredients" markdown="1">
- 1 russet potato: olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika, onion powder
- 2 eggs: salt, pepper, onion powder
- a bit of milk
- some grated cheese to finish
</div>

<cook-mode-toggle></cook-mode-toggle>

## Steps

1. Dice the potato, rinse in water, dry, then toss in oil, paprika, salt, etc.
2. Cook in cast iron for about 5 minutes with the lid on, then flip, then cook another 5 or so.
3. Pour in the egg-milk mixture.
4. Finish with grated cheese.
